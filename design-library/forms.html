Accessibility Guidelines

✓ Do
  • Always pair inputs with visible labels
  • Use placeholder text to show format, not as a label replacement
  • Ensure focus states are clearly visible
  • Provide clear error messages near the relevant field
✗ Don't
  • Rely on placeholder text as the only label
  • Disable the browser's default focus outline without a replacement
  • Use color alone to indicate errors
  • Make touch targets smaller than 44x44px
